What Unfret does

Unfret checks product pages for shops that may not deliver, products that may not work, generic goods sold at a brand's price and pushy tricks such as fake countdowns. It tells you how risky the page looks, from 0 to 4, and why, in plain words.

Free, with no account

Quick and deep checks are free. There is no account and no sign-in: no check is tied to you. Free checks run on donations.

What is sent, and when

Only when you click “Check this page” or “Quick check (free)”: the product facts and the cleaned visible text of that page go to unfret.net. Never anything you typed or anything that says who you are. Page addresses aren't stored, nothing is sold and there are no affiliate links. Product prices are kept anonymously for 90 days to spot fake discounts. A deep check first does a few seconds of math in your browser, which keeps the free checks from being abused.

On a marketplace ad or a service provider's page, that includes the ad (never a private seller's name) or the business details the provider publishes.

What Unfret does

Unfret checks product pages for scam shops, generic factory goods sold as a brand, fake discounts and pushy tricks such as fake countdowns, and explains what it found in plain words.

What is sent, and when

Only when you click “Check this page”: the product facts and the cleaned visible text of that page go to unfret.net. Never your account details or anything you typed. Page addresses aren't stored, nothing is sold and there are no affiliate links. Product prices are kept anonymously for 90 days to spot fake discounts. Once, when you installed Unfret, it asked unfret.net for your Unfret pass with its free checks; that carried nothing about you or your browsing.

On a marketplace ad or a service provider's page, that includes the ad (never a private seller's name) or the business details the provider publishes.

Paying for checks

Sign in with a Solana wallet, or use an Unfret pass, and use prepaid checks.

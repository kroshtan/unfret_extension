Privacy

Unfret reads a page only when you click "Check this page". It then sends the product facts and the page's visible product text (without account menus, addresses or forms) to the Unfret server. It stores only your sign-in session or API key, your consent and these settings in your browser, and no analysis history. A page is shared for review only when you report a result as wrong and agree. A result becomes a public link only when you share it and confirm what the preview shows; that link names no account.

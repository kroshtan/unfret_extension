Privacy

Unfret reads a page only when you click "Check this page" or "Quick check (free)". It then sends the product facts and the page's visible product text (without account menus, your addresses or forms) to the Unfret server, with nothing that says who you are. It stores only your consent and these settings in your browser, and no analysis history. A result becomes a public link only when you share it and confirm what the preview shows; that link names no one.

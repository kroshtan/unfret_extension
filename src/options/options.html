Account

Not signed in.

Server

The default is https://veridex.kroshtan.com. For development, http://localhost or http://127.0.0.1 (any port) can be used; the browser asks you to allow access to it.

Privacy

Veridex reads a page only when you click "Analyse this page". It then sends the product facts and the page's visible product text (without account menus, addresses or forms) to the Veridex server. It stores only your sign-in session and these settings in your browser, and no analysis history.

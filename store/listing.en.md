## Description

Is this shop legit? Is this seller real? Unfret checks the product page you're on and tells you, in plain words, whether anything about it should worry you, before you pay.

Fake sellers on real platforms
On Amazon, Walmart and other big marketplaces the platform is real, but the seller may not be. Unfret looks at who actually sells the product: a seller that isn't the brand, the same product sold elsewhere for much less, a price far below what other shops ask, reviews copied from other shops or written about another product.

Shops that aren't what they seem
Everywhere else, Unfret is a fake shop checker and a fake discount checker in one click:
• Fake countdowns that restart when you reload the page
• "Was" prices nobody ever paid, and discounts too good to be true
• Dropshipping: generic factory goods sold as something special, the same product photo on other shops, long hidden delivery times
• Made-up brand names and product titles stuffed with search terms
• Brand-new web shops, missing company details and no return policy
• Reviews that look bought, copied or about another product
• Paid extras ticked for you in advance, and costs that only appear at checkout

Four plain answers, not a score
Every check ends in one of four answers: Looks fine, Worth a look, Think twice or Serious concerns. Under it: what Unfret found and where it found it (the page itself, the domain registry, the brand's own website), what it checked, and what you can do next. Unfret never calls a shop a scam. It shows what it found, and you decide. When a page looks fine, Unfret says so too, and shows what looked good: a known brand, sold by the brand itself, natural-looking reviews, an established shop.

How accurate is it? The numbers for each answer, tested on real pages: https://unfret.net/accuracy

Unfret also checks ads on second-hand marketplaces and the websites of tradespeople and other local services.

Free to start
• 5 free deep checks when you install Unfret. No account, no e-mail, no code.
• After that, quick checks stay free, as many as you like: they read the page itself, by Unfret's rules.
• Deep checks, which also look the seller, the shop and the product up elsewhere, come in packs you buy on unfret.net, by card or with crypto (USDC or SOL): 25 checks for $6 ($5 with crypto) or 100 checks for $20, plus sales tax or VAT where it applies; in the euro area €6 or €20, VAT included. No subscription, and checks never expire.
• Every pack comes with gift codes to share, one per 25 checks bought (1 with 25 checks, 4 with 100), 5 checks each. A gift code from a friend works for any Unfret pass, new or old, except the one that bought the pack (one gift code a month per pass).
Your checks are held by your Unfret pass, an anonymous key the extension makes for you; no account or wallet needed. Paid with a Solana wallet? Sign in with it instead: signing in is a free message, not a transaction, and Unfret never gets access to your funds.

Private by design
• Nothing is read or sent until you click "Check this page".
• Only the product facts and the cleaned visible text of that one page are sent; account menus, your addresses, forms and anything you typed are removed in your browser first. The page address goes without tracking parameters, sessions or anything after the #.
• The pages you check aren't stored and are never linked to you or to your IP address. A result becomes public only if you share it.
• No account, no tracking, no ads, no affiliate links to shops, no browsing history.

Share a result: turn a check into a link to send to someone. Unfret shows you exactly what the public page will say before anything is shared. The page has no account attached and never says who checked; it stays up for 90 days, and you get a code to delete it sooner.

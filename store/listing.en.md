## Description

Shopping online shouldn't feel like a gamble. Unfret checks the product page you're looking at and tells you, in plain words, whether anything about it should worry you.

Click the Unfret button on a product page and you get one of four answers (Looks fine, Worth a look, Think twice, or Serious concerns) with the reasons behind it:

• Fake countdowns that restart when you reload the page
• "Was" prices nobody ever paid, and discounts too good to be true
• Made-up brand names and generic factory goods sold as something special
• Product titles stuffed with search terms
• Brand-new web shops, missing company details and no return policy
• Reviews that look bought, copied or about another product
• Paid extras ticked for you in advance, and costs that only appear at checkout

When a page looks fine, Unfret says so too, and shows what looked good: a known brand, sold by the brand itself, natural-looking reviews, an established shop.

Share a result: turn a check into a link to send to someone. Unfret shows you exactly what the public page will say before anything is shared. The page has no account attached and never says who checked; it stays up for 90 days, and you get a code to delete it sooner.

Private by design
• Nothing is read or sent until you click "Check this page".
• Only the product facts and the cleaned visible text of that one page are sent; account menus, your addresses, forms and anything you typed are removed in your browser first.
• The pages you check aren't stored and are never linked to you. A result becomes public only if you share it.
• No tracking, no ads, no affiliate links, no browsing history.

How you pay
Checks are prepaid, in packs of 25, 50 or 100 (20 cents a check with USDC or SOL, a little more by card, plus VAT where it applies). Every pack comes with invites to give away, one per 25 checks: 25 checks + 1 invite of 3 checks for a friend, 50 + 2 invites, 100 + 4 invites. An invite adds 3 checks to a new wallet or API key, and isn't linked to whoever bought the pack. Sign in with a Solana wallet, or use an API key, which you can also top up by card. Unfret never gets access to your funds: signing in is a free message, not a transaction.

Works on Amazon, bol.com and most web shops. The code is open source (MIT).

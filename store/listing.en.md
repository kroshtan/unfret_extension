## Description

Is this shop legit? Unfret checks the product page you're looking at and tells you, in plain words, whether anything about it should worry you: a fake shop checker and fake discount checker in one click, for web shops, marketplace sellers and ads.

Click the Unfret button on a product page and you get one of four answers (Looks fine, Worth a look, Think twice, or Serious concerns) with the reasons behind it:

• Fake countdowns that restart when you reload the page
• "Was" prices nobody ever paid, and fake discounts too good to be true
• Dropshipping: generic factory goods sold as something special, with long hidden delivery times
• Made-up brand names and product titles stuffed with search terms
• Brand-new web shops, missing company details and no return policy
• Reviews that look bought, copied or about another product
• Paid extras ticked for you in advance, and costs that only appear at checkout

Unfret never calls a shop a scam. It shows what it found and where it found it, and you decide. When a page looks fine, Unfret says so too, and shows what looked good: a known brand, sold by the brand itself, natural-looking reviews, an established shop.

Looking for a Fakespot alternative? Unfret looks at more than the reviews: the price history, the shop behind the listing, the product itself and the tricks on the page.

How accurate is it? The numbers for each verdict, tested on real pages: https://unfret.net/accuracy

Share a result: turn a check into a link to send to someone. Unfret shows you exactly what the public page will say before anything is shared. The page has no account attached and never says who checked; it stays up for 90 days, and you get a code to delete it sooner.

Private by design
• Nothing is read or sent until you click "Check this page".
• Only the product facts and the cleaned visible text of that one page are sent; account menus, your addresses, forms and anything you typed are removed in your browser first. The page address goes without tracking parameters, sessions or anything after the #.
• The pages you check aren't stored and are never linked to you. A result becomes public only if you share it.
• No account, no tracking, no ads, no affiliate links to shops, no browsing history.

How you pay
• Free to start: 5 free deep checks when you install Unfret. No code, no account, no e-mail.
• After that, quick checks stay free: they read the page itself, by Unfret's rules.
• Deep checks, which also look the shop and the product up elsewhere, come in packs of 25, 50 or 100 that you buy on unfret.net, by card or with crypto (USDC or SOL): about 20 cents a check, a little more by card, plus VAT where it applies. No subscription, and checks never expire.
• Every pack comes with gift codes to share, one per 25 checks bought (4 with a pack of 100), 5 checks each. A gift code from a friend works for any Unfret pass, new or old, except the one that bought the pack (one gift code a month per pass).
Your checks are held by your Unfret pass, an anonymous key the extension makes for you; no wallet or account needed. Paid with a Solana wallet? Sign in with it instead: signing in is a free message, not a transaction, and Unfret never gets access to your funds.

Works on big marketplaces (third-party sellers included), private-seller ad sites and most web shops. The code is open source (MIT).

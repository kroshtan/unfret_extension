## Description

Is this shop legit? Is this seller real? Unfret checks the product page you're on and tells you, in plain words, how risky it looks and why, before you pay. Free, with no account.

Fake sellers on real platforms
On Amazon, Walmart and other big marketplaces the platform is real, but the seller may not be. Unfret looks at who actually sells the product: a seller that isn't the brand, the same product sold elsewhere for much less, a price far below what other shops ask, reviews copied from other shops or written about another product.

Shops that aren't what they seem
Everywhere else, Unfret is a fake shop checker and a fake discount checker in one click:
• Fake countdowns that restart when you reload the page
• "Was" prices nobody ever paid, and discounts too good to be true
• Dropshipping: generic factory goods sold as something special, the same product photo on other shops, long hidden delivery times
• Made-up brand names and claims that don't add up
• Brand-new web shops, missing company details and no return policy
• Reviews that look bought, copied or about another product
• Paid extras ticked for you in advance, and costs that only appear at checkout

One risk level, and why
Every check ends in one risk level from 0 to 4: no concerns found, pressure and tricks, may cost more than it's worth, may not work as described, or may not arrive or may not be safe. Under it: every finding behind that level, worst first, and where Unfret found it (the page itself, the domain registry, the brand's own website). Unfret never calls a shop a scam. It shows what it found, and you decide. When a page looks fine, Unfret says so too, and shows what looked good.

How accurate is it? The numbers, tested on real pages: https://unfret.net/accuracy

Unfret also checks ads on second-hand marketplaces and the websites of tradespeople and other local services.

Free for shoppers
• Quick checks and deep checks are free. No account, no sign-in, no e-mail, no pass.
• A quick check reads the page itself, by Unfret's rules. A deep check also looks the seller, the shop and the product up elsewhere.
• Free checks run on donations. If the day's share runs out, quick checks keep working and deep checks are back tomorrow. Support Unfret: https://unfret.net/support

Wrong result?
Tell Unfret which risk level you think is right and which reasons are wrong or missing. Your report keeps the page it's about, with personal details removed, so a person can review it. It never keeps who sent it. If you like, turn on "Count my reports" to see how many of your reports agreed with the final answer, and pick a nickname for the leaderboard.

Private by design
• Nothing is read or sent until you click "Check this page".
• Only the product facts and the cleaned visible text of that one page are sent; account menus, your addresses, forms and anything you typed are removed in your browser first. The page address goes without tracking parameters, sessions or anything after the #.
• No check is tied to you: no account, no pass, no device ID. The pages you check aren't stored and are never linked to you or to your IP address.
• No tracking, no ads, no affiliate links to shops, no browsing history.

Share a result: turn a check into a link to send to someone. Unfret shows you exactly what the public page will say before anything is shared. The page never says who checked; it stays up for 90 days, and you get a code to delete it sooner.

The code is open source (MIT).
